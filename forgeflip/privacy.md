# Privacy Policy: ForgeFlip

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026
**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information ForgeFlip ("the game", "we") collects, how it's used, and how to delete it. ForgeFlip is published by Tiny Shapes Arcade, a one-person studio operated by Jonathon Pifher. It applies to the Android app and the browser version at forgeflip.fly.dev.

Questions? Use **Report a problem** in the game's Settings, or email **jonathon@tinyshapesarcade.com**.

---

## The short version

- You can play ForgeFlip without giving us anything. Everything about your game stays on your device.
- If you choose to post scores to the online leaderboard, we store the **name you pick** and your **scores**.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We don't sell data.
- The age check asks for your birth month and year, checks it on your device, and never saves or sends it.
- You can delete your leaderboard name and every score posted with it at any time, from inside the game.

---

## What stays on your device

Your settings, best scores, run history, embers, unlocked looks, achievements, missions and tutorial progress are saved only on your device. We never see them. They're removed when you uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data.

## What we ask for and do not keep

**Your birth month and year.** ForgeFlip is for players 13 and older, so the first time you open the game it asks when you were born. The game checks it once, on your device, and then discards it. It is never sent to us, never written to your device's storage, and we keep nothing worked out from it, so nobody, including us, can look it up later. The game only remembers that you agreed to the terms (and which version). If the check finds you're under 13, your device keeps a marker for one day that says only that the check was refused; it holds no date or age.

## What we collect (only if you use the leaderboard)

The first time you post a score, the game asks you to pick a leaderboard name. From then on, the game sends our server:

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your leaderboard name** (3–12 characters, chosen by you) | Shown to other players next to your scores |
| **A random sign-in token** made by your device | Proves score posts come from you. The server stores only a one-way hash of it, never the token itself |
| **Your scores**: score, lap reached, play time, close calls, and similar run stats | Ranking you on the all-time and Trial by Fire leaderboards, and checking that scores are possible (anti-cheat) |
| **Names you report** as inappropriate | So we can review them |

## Reports you send us

If you use **Report a problem**, we keep what you wrote (the kind of report, a short summary and the details), which screen of the game you were on, whether you used the app or a browser, and the game's version. If you choose to give an email address for a reply, it's kept with that report only and used only to answer it. You don't need a leaderboard name to send a report.

Your leaderboard name is the only thing other players can see. Don't use your real name, or any personal details, as your leaderboard name.

## Technical information

Like any website, our server receives your **IP address** when the game talks to it. We use it only to limit abuse (for example, too many requests in a minute). It's held in memory for at most an hour and is not saved with your scores. Our security logs record a scrambled, one-way version of it that can't be turned back into the address and changes every time the server restarts.

## What we do NOT collect

- Your real name, email, phone number, or contacts
- Your location, photos, camera, or microphone
- Advertising IDs or other device identifiers
- Your activity in other apps or websites
- Payment information (the game is free)

## Who sees it

- **Other players** see your leaderboard name and scores, and nothing else.
- **The person who runs ForgeFlip** sees leaderboard data, name reports and problem reports when moderating or fixing something.
- **Fly.io** (fly.io), which hosts our server and its data, sees the requests your game makes, including your IP address.

Nobody else.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else. Fly.io's privacy policy: https://fly.io/legal/privacy-policy/

## How long we keep it

- **Leaderboard name and scores** (all-time and Trial by Fire): until you delete them (see below), or until we remove a name that breaks our rules.
- **Name reports:** until reviewed; removed when the reported name is deleted.
- **Problem reports** (and any email given with one): until the problem is dealt with, and no longer than 12 months (older ones are deleted automatically).
- **Security logs:** kept by our hosting provider for a limited time, then deleted.

## Deleting your data

- **In the game:** Settings → **Delete my leaderboard name**. This deletes your name, every score posted with it, and any problem reports you sent while using it from our server right away, and the name becomes free for others.
- **By Report a problem or email:** if you can't delete it in the game, choose "About my data" in Report a problem, or email **jonathon@tinyshapesarcade.com**, with your leaderboard name, and we'll delete it within 30 days. You can also ask us what we hold about your name the same way.
- **On your device:** uninstall the app, or clear the site data in your browser.

## Children

ForgeFlip is for players 13 and older, and isn't directed at children under 13. The birth month and year asked for when the game first opens is how that's checked; as set out above, it's used for that check alone and is never stored or sent. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has a leaderboard name, tell us and it will be removed along with its scores.

## Security

All communication with our server is encrypted (HTTPS only). Sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. See the game's security notes for more detail.

## Changes to this policy

If anything material changes (for example, if we add advertising), we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change goes live, and mention it in the game's release notes.

## Report a problem

Questions, complaints about how your data is handled, and data requests all come through **Report a problem** in the game's Settings, or **jonathon@tinyshapesarcade.com**. No leaderboard name needed.
