# ForgeFlip

**A one-button arcade runner set in a blacksmith's forge.** Tap to flip your spark between the floor and the ceiling. Every flip strikes a glowing bar that becomes a wall on the next lap, so the forge you build is the forge you have to survive.

- Smash anvils with last-second **strikes**
- Shave past danger for **close calls**, chain them into a **×10 combo** and set off white-hot **Fever**
- Stay off glowing coals and molten metal, and flip from blue **quench** to leave no wall behind
- Charge an **ability** with close calls and unleash it when you're ready
- Face **forge events** from lap 11: Ember Rush, Hammer Storm, Blackout, Topsy-Turvy and more
- Catch floating **embers** to unlock new spark colors and bar metals
- Survive **Trial by Fire**: the hard mode, where even stone burns, hotter every lap
- Climb the global **leaderboard**
