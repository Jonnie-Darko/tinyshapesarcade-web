# Terms of Service: ForgeFlip

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

ForgeFlip is a one-button arcade game. These rules are about playing fair and treating other players decently. By playing ForgeFlip, you agree to them.

## Who runs ForgeFlip

ForgeFlip is made and run by Tiny Shapes Arcade, a one-person studio operated by Jonathon Pifher. "We" and "us" on these pages mean Tiny Shapes Arcade.

To reach us about anything, including a complaint, an appeal or a legal notice, use Report a problem in the game's Settings, or write to jonathon@tinyshapesarcade.com. You don't need a leaderboard name to do either.

## Who can play

You must be 13 or older to play ForgeFlip. The first time you open the game, it asks for the month and year you were born, and asks you to agree to these terms. The date is checked once, on your device, to make sure you are old enough. It is never sent to us and never saved, so we can't look it up, show it or share it later. If the date makes you younger than 13, the game can't be played and that device can't try again for a day, so check the date before you continue. Giving a false date to get around the age limit breaks these rules, and a leaderboard name found to belong to someone under 13 is removed along with its scores.

## 1. Play fair

Every score must come from you, playing by hand, in the game itself. Modified versions of the game, scripts, bots, auto-tappers and macros are not allowed. Neither is faking, editing or replaying scores, tampering with what the game sends to our server, exploiting a bug instead of reporting it, or attacking the service itself.

No AI or software may play for you. That includes AI assistants and agents, browser extensions that read the screen or tap for you, and computer-use or automation tools of any kind. Using an AI to read these rules or the help is fine; letting one play is not. Nobody can grant an exception.

Scores we believe weren't earned fairly are removed, and the name that posted them may be banned from the leaderboard.

## 2. Your leaderboard name

A leaderboard name is optional; you only need one to post scores online. One name each. Names may not be sold, traded or shared.

Names must not be offensive, hateful, sexual or threatening, must not impersonate anyone else (including us), and must not contain anyone's personal details, such as a full real name, address or phone number. Don't use your own real name. We can remove a name that breaks these rules, along with its scores.

You can delete your name and every score posted with it at any time: Settings → Delete my leaderboard name.

## 3. Reports and enforcement

Use the flag on the leaderboard to report a name, or Report a problem for cheating, bugs or anything else. Reports are read by a person; please don't send the same one twice. Depending on what happened, the result is a warning, a name or score being removed, or a ban from the leaderboard. Decisions can be appealed once by email and are then final.
